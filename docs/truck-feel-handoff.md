# Truck steering, seat exit and dolly placement

Integrates Claude A's claude/truck-feel commits through 245127c without editing their client code. The dolly follows floor-height footsteps sampled every 0.5 studs, tilts to their slope up to 45 degrees, and may shift its target sideways up to 1.5 studs when its footprint intersects a jamb. Slowdown measures the load's miss from its own target, with a one-stud allowance for normal following lag; walking away from a snagged load stops at full slack and walking back remains full dolly speed. Config mirrors all active values and removes unused DollyTurnRate.

Steering servo response and straight-line correction reduce drift when driving without input. Jump remains locked at speed and while a jump is held through stopping, then restores on exit. Dolly loads use a 1.1-stud deck and quarter-turn rotation. Grab prechecks use the same grip attachment range as the current server.

Claude A measured approximately 0.4 degrees drift / 2.1 studs lateral motion over 187 studs for steering.
