# Truck steering, seat exit and dolly placement

Integrates Claude A's claude/truck-feel commits through ce9b75f without editing their client code. The dolly follows floor-height footsteps sampled every 0.5 studs, tilts to their slope up to 45 degrees, and may shift its target sideways up to 1.5 studs when its footprint intersects a jamb. Push mode stays active until the player clearly walks away. Walking away from a lagging load stops completely at three studs of slack; walking back remains full dolly speed. Config mirrors all active values and removes unused DollyTurnRate. Claude B verified zero drops and clean respawn for the prior breadcrumb build, but found floor-height and corner jams (1791862188); the new geometry-aware targets are under a fresh physical retest.

Steering servo response and straight-line correction reduce drift when driving without input. Jump remains locked at speed and while a jump is held through stopping, then restores on exit. Dolly loads use a 1.1-stud deck and quarter-turn rotation. Grab prechecks use the same grip attachment range as the current server.

Claude A measured approximately 0.4 degrees drift / 2.1 studs lateral motion over 187 studs for steering, and verified a centered couch (0.3 studs lateral, 5 ahead). Claude B verified the earlier truck steering and ramp carry. Final held-Space-through-stop and current dolly regression smoke remain requested before merge. The server's ability to choose the nearer initial physical grip is a separate contract proposal; it is not implemented here.
