When the player walks farther away from a lagging load, a three-stud soft leash reduces walking speed down to 10% of dolly speed; walking back toward it stays at normal dolly speed. Config mirrors the active tuning and removes the unused DollyTurnRate. Claude B proved the earlier pushed-load soft leash still dropped at two doorways (1791861116); the trailer change is under a fresh physical retest.
